# Phase 4: Series and virality design

## 1. Series architecture

| Layer | Plan |
|---|---|
| **Seasons** | 1 season = 1 state/UT, in the clockwise journey order (`01-research/states-and-districts.md`). 36 seasons and 793 district episodes. |
| **Episodes** | 1 Short per district, 35–55 s, 9:16. |
| **Season opener** | A 30 s "Welcome to [State]" Short: map flyover, how many districts, one teaser per sub-region. |
| **Season finale** | A long-form compilation (8–15 min, 16:9 or 9:16) of every district in the state, plus a "Rocket Mode" highlights reel. This is the main source of watch hours (see monetization). |
| **Lore drops** | At milestones (district 50, 100, 250, 500, 793) and state crossings. |
| **Viewer requests** | "Invite Seetu": viewers nominate their family's real meal. Picks are re-verified before airing, which adds real local voices over time. |

**Pacing:**
- **Launch:** 1 Short per day for the first 8 weeks, which covers Ladakh, J&K and part of HP.
- **Scale:** 2 per day once the pipeline is stable and verification keeps up. **Never let posting speed outrun verification.**
- At 2 per day, the full map takes about 13 months, plus long-forms.

**Binge hooks built in:**
- The cliffhanger always names the next district, and it is the real neighbour.
- The district counter (N/793) and the map line grow visibly.
- Ongoing gags: Rocket Mode, "Every day… or just for guests?", the extra-helping ambush.
- Each Short's **Related video** link points to that state's playlist or long-form.

## 2. The fixed episode format (45 s target; 35–55 s allowed)

| Time | Beat | What happens | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0.0–2.0 s** | **HOOK** | Whistle and steam burst, lid-pop, eyes to the camera. Hook line = the episode's most surprising *verified* fact. Hook text top third. | Single whistle **WHEET!** then VO |
| 2.0–4.5 s | Arrival | "What's cooking, *[District]*?" Seetu arrives by local transport over the establishing background; map pin drops. | Transport SFX |
| 4.5–14 s | **BREAKFAST** | 2–3 quick cooking shots (ingredients → vessel → plate). Dish card. Steam-o-meter (spice, 0–3 puffs). | Breakfast bed, 1 whistle at the end |
| 14–24 s | **LUNCH** | Same structure. The "Every day… or just for guests?" beat sits here or at dinner. | Lunch bed, 2 whistles |
| 24–34 s | **DINNER** | Same structure. The extra-helping ambush sits here. | Dinner bed, 3 whistles |
| 34–41 s | **UNIQUE FACT** | Lid-pop, fact card with a tiny source tag, one supporting visual (diagram, map or object). | Music ducks, riser, "ding" |
| 41–45 s | **CLIFFHANGER** | "Three whistles — done! Just one more… *[Next district]*!" A teaser silhouette of the next dish or vehicle, stamp and counter. Last frame matches the first frame for a smooth loop. | Train-chug build, cut on the whistle |

**Script rules:**
- 115–130 spoken words.
- **Every factual sentence carries a row ID** from the data table (e.g. `KL-ALP-L`) in the script margin.
- Only *Verified* facts are stated flatly. *Partially verified* facts are phrased as regional ("Across central Kerala…"). *Unverified* items are never stated.

**Variety rule (to avoid YouTube's "inauthentic content" flag):**
- Mass-produced, repetitive content is demonetized channel-wide (policy renamed 15 Jul 2025, P08).
- So each episode must change **at least 3 of these 6**:
  - hook type (number, question, myth-bust, sound)
  - arrival vehicle
  - which gag is used
  - camera framing
  - which meal the "guests" beat lands on
  - fact visual type
- Original research and narration are the core value; the template is only the skeleton.

## 3. Titles, thumbnails, hashtags

**Title formulas** (≤ 60 characters; district and "India" always present):
- Fact-first: `They grow rice BELOW sea level | Alappuzha, India 🇮🇳`
- Day-of-eating: `What a family in Leh, India eats in a day`
- Question: `Would you drink SALTY tea? | Leh, Ladakh`
- Counter (for bingers): `Kohima, Nagaland | District 154/793 🍚`

Rotate the formulas, and A/B test titles where YouTube allows (Test & Compare).

**Thumbnails / cover frames:**
- Custom Shorts thumbnails are available to YPP creators (July 2026 rollout, P11). Until then, choose a strong frame in the mobile app.
- Recipe: Seetu's face (an emotion) + the hero dish + up to 3 words ("SALTY TEA?!").
- Keep the same layout every time so the channel page reads as a series.

**Hashtags:**
- 3–5 per Short; the first 3 show above the title.
- Fixed: `#WhatIndiaEats`
- Rotating: `#[State]` `#IndianFood` `#[District]` `#DayOfEating`
- Don't stack 15 hashtags.

## 4. Posting frequency and times

- **Frequency:** 1 per day at launch → 2 per day once stable. Add 1 long-form compilation at each season finale.
- **Starting times (hypothesis):**
  - **Slot A:** 14:30 UTC (20:00 IST · 14:30 UK · 09:30 US-Eastern · 18:30 Gulf). Hits Indian evening prime time, the Gulf evening and the UK afternoon, and Shorts keep distributing into the US day.
  - **Slot B (when doing 2 per day):** 23:00 UTC (18:00 US-Eastern · 15:00 US-Pacific).
- **Why it's only a hypothesis:** published "best time" studies conflict (P23 and others). After 30 days, switch to YouTube Studio's *When your viewers are on YouTube* report and test one slot change at a time.

## 6. Comment-bait that also helps the research (no fake controversy)

- "What's breakfast in **your** country? Seetu is taking notes 📝"
- "Locals: did we get your district right? Correct us. We check every comment against sources."
  - This builds trust and is a free lead generator for verification. A comment alone is never a source; it is a lead to check.
- "Rate the spice: 1, 2 or 3 whistles? 🔥"
- "Guess tomorrow's breakfast in [next district] 👇" (pin the winner's comment the next day)
- "How does your grandma say 'eat one more!' in your language?" (this also collects local phrases for future gags)
- "Which plate would you eat: breakfast, lunch or dinner?"

Pin one question per Short. Reply to the first 30 minutes of comments personally, because that early engagement helps the Short spread.

TikTok is not available to creators in India. Add it only if the channel operates from elsewhere.
