# States, UTs and district counts (checked 18 Nov 2026)

**Total: 28 states + 8 union territories = 791 administrative districts** (740 in states, 51 in UTs).
**Series episodes: 793 district episodes.** Puducherry's Mahé and Yanam each get an episode because their food is culturally distinct, even though they are census districts, not administrative ones.

Why the national total differs between sources (784, 791, 802, 806):
- Some lists count *proposed* districts. Examples: West Bengal's 2022 and 2026 plans, Uttarakhand's 2011 announcements, Madhya Pradesh's Pichhore.
- Some lists count Puducherry's census districts.
- Some lists predate the late-2025 and 2026 changes.

The changes found and verified since early 2025 are in the table below. **A Census 2027 boundary freeze has been in force since 1 Jan 2026** (no district, block or village changes until 31 Mar 2027; sources D24, D25). That explains why almost nothing changed in 2026 except Ladakh.

Machine-readable version: `districts-master.csv`. Source IDs (D01–D32) are listed in `sources.md`.

## Order chosen: a clockwise "journey" route, not alphabetical

The series starts at the top of the map (Ladakh) and runs down through the north and east into the Northeast. It then comes down the east coast, crosses to the islands, comes back up the west coast, and **ends in Madhya Pradesh, the "heart of India"**.

Reasons for this order:
1. **Every cliffhanger is real.** Nearly every "next district" is the actual neighbour, so viewers get the comparison you asked for in Phase 1 ("what makes this district different from its neighbours"). The few non-adjacent hops become travel moments: a long train ride, the ship from Chennai to Port Blair, the boat from Kerala to Lakshadweep.
2. **It opens with a strong first impression for global viewers.** Ladakh and Kashmir have Himalayan landscapes and food that breaks stereotypes (salty butter tea, barley).
3. **It gives a clear progress bar.** A dotted line moves round the map, and a counter reads "District 37 of 793", which suits a binge-watching audience.
4. **District order inside each state** follows a "snake" path through neighbouring districts. It starts with the district nearest the previous state and ends with the one nearest the next state. Kerala, for example, runs south to north: Thiruvananthapuram → … → Kasaragod, then the boat to Lakshadweep.

Alphabetical order was rejected because it jumps across the country (Andhra → Arunachal → Assam) and gives no story. If you prefer it, the data and skill support it; it only changes the sort key.
