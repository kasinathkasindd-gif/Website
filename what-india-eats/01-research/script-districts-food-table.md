## 1. Leh (Ladakh (UT))

**Unique fact** — ✅ Verified  
Butter tea (gur-gur cha) is salty, not sweet: tea churned with butter and salt. Rice was once a luxury in Ladakh; subsidised rice from the plains is now a staple, partly displacing local barley (S69; debated in S70). Leh became one of 8 Ladakh districts in 2026. [S68; S69; S70; S72; S73]

### Breakfast: Khambir + Gur-gur cha (butter tea); or Kholak (ngamphe/tsampa mixed into butter tea)
- **English:** Thick local wheat bread with salty butter tea; or roasted-barley-flour dough made with the tea
- **Ingredients:** Khambir: whole-wheat flour, water, yeast/sourdough, salt. Butter tea: brick or black tea, water, salt, butter, milk. Kholak: roasted barley flour (ngamphe), butter tea
- **Method:** Khambir dough rested and baked or griddled into a thick round. Tea boiled long, then churned with butter and salt in a tall wooden churn (or blender) until frothy. Kholak: tea poured onto barley flour and kneaded by hand - no cooking.
- **Status:** 🟡 Partially verified · **Data basis:** District-specific (Ladakh) · **Sources:** S68; S71
- **Notes:** Only one countable source (S68, academic); S71 is a travel operator (pointer only). Needs a second tier A-C source or a local reviewer. Kholak and butter tea are described as quick, low-fuel breakfasts by IJTK (S68). Traditional pattern - many modern Leh homes also eat bread, eggs or paratha.
- **Spice (editorial):** 0 steam puffs (0-3)

### Lunch: Daal-chawal (rice + dal) + Vegetables; traditional alternative: Skyu
- **English:** Rice and lentils with seasonal vegetables; or skyu, thumb-pressed wheat pasta in vegetable/meat stew
- **Ingredients:** Rice (often subsidised PDS rice), lentils, potato, turnip, greens; skyu: wheat flour, water, vegetables, sometimes meat or dried cheese
- **Method:** Rice boiled or pressure-cooked; dal simmered with tomato and spices; skyu dough pinched into small thumb-pressed discs and simmered in the stew until cooked (~20-30 min).
- **Status:** 🟡 Partially verified · **Data basis:** District-specific (rice shift) + traditional pattern · **Sources:** S69; S70; S71
- **Notes:** The rice-for-barley shift is documented (S69) but its causes are debated (S70: market demand vs subsidy). Skyu is from travel sources only - confirm locally.
- **Spice (editorial):** 1 steam puffs (0-3)

### Dinner: Thukpa
- **English:** Noodle soup with vegetables and/or meat
- **Ingredients:** Wheat noodles (hand-made or bought), onion, garlic, ginger, turnip/greens, sometimes mutton; salt
- **Method:** Broth simmered with vegetables (and meat); hand-pulled or rolled noodles cooked in it (~20-30 min).
- **Status:** 🟡 Partially verified · **Data basis:** Regional pattern: Ladakh · **Sources:** S71; S68
- **Notes:** Dinner pattern is the least documented; travel sources describe thukpa and skyu as daily staples - confirm with local reviewer.
- **Spice (editorial):** 1 steam puffs (0-3)

## 2. Kohima (Nagaland)

**Unique fact** — ✅ Verified  
Naga kitchens preserve meat by smoking it on racks above the hearth for days to weeks. The Naga King Chilli (Raja Mircha) got a GI tag in 2008 and measures about 1,000,000 Scoville units - over 100x a jalapeno. [S76; S77; S80; S78]

### Breakfast: Morning rice meal: Rice + boiled seasonal greens + Tathu (chilli chutney)
- **English:** Rice with simply boiled greens and a fresh chilli chutney - many Naga homes eat a full rice meal in the morning
- **Ingredients:** Rice; mustard leaves or other greens; chutney of king chilli or other chilli, tomato, salt (sometimes fermented soybean or dried fish)
- **Method:** Rice boiled; greens boiled with salt (little or no oil); chilli and tomato roasted or pounded with salt in a mortar.
- **Status:** 🟡 Partially verified · **Data basis:** Regional pattern: Angami / Naga · **Sources:** S74; S81
- **Notes:** 'Rice up to twice a day' (Angami) comes from secondary pages - confirm with a local reviewer before saying 'breakfast is a full rice meal' on screen.
- **Spice (editorial):** 2-3 steam puffs (0-3)

### Lunch: Galho
- **English:** Angami one-pot rice porridge with vegetables and smoked pork
- **Ingredients:** Rice, water, seasonal greens, smoked pork, ginger, chilli, salt (fermented soybean optional)
- **Method:** Rice simmered in plenty of water with chopped smoked pork and greens until thick and porridge-like (~30-40 min).
- **Status:** 🟡 Partially verified · **Data basis:** Regional pattern: Angami / Chakhesang · **Sources:** S74; S81
- **Notes:** Midday eating habits vary (tea + snacks in many homes) - not verified.
- **Spice (editorial):** 2 steam puffs (0-3)

### Dinner: Rice + Smoked pork with bamboo shoot (or with axone) + Boiled vegetables + Chutney
- **English:** Rice with smoked pork cooked with fermented bamboo shoot (or fermented soybean), boiled greens and chilli chutney
- **Ingredients:** Smoked pork, fermented bamboo shoot or axone (fermented soybean), ginger, garlic, king chilli, salt; greens; rice
- **Method:** Smoked pork simmered with bamboo shoot (or axone), ginger and chilli until tender (~45-60 min); greens boiled; rice boiled.
- **Status:** 🟡 Partially verified · **Data basis:** Regional pattern: Naga (axone more Sumi/Ao/Lotha than Angami) · **Sources:** S75; S77; S80; S74
- **Notes:** Pork is common but not universal; describe it factually without value words.
- **Spice (editorial):** 3 (Rocket if raja mircha) steam puffs (0-3)
