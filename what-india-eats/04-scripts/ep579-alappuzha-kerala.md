## Clean VO

> This family farms… *below* sea level. What's cooking, Alappuzha? Breakfast across Kerala: puttu, rice flour and coconut steamed in a tube, with black-chickpea curry. Spice: two puffs! Lunch: red matta rice, fish curry soured with kudampuli, a dried fruit called Malabar tamarind, and a coconut stir-fry. Here, the rice and the fish come from the same waters. Karimeen? Every day… or just for guests? Everyday fish here is often sardine or mackerel. No no no… okay. Just one more. Dinner? Often kappa, boiled tapioca, with that same fish curry. Duck curry is for special days. Mind blown: farmers here drained a wetland, built mud walls and grow rice below sea level. The UN's FAO calls it a world agricultural heritage system. After harvest, the ducks move in. Three whistles — done! Just one more… Kottayam! Where they say the fish curry tastes better on day two.

**Pronunciation:**
- puttu → "PUTT-oo"
- kudampuli → "koo-DUM-poo-lee"
- matta → "MUT-tah"
- karimeen → "KAH-ree-meen"
- kappa → "KUP-pah"
- Alappuzha → "AH-lup-poo-zha", with the soft Malayalam 'zh' (close to a soft "r/l"). Coach the TTS with a respelling and check it with a Kerala reviewer; this is the most-mispronounced name in the series.
