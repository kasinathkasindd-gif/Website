## Clean VO (for TTS or recording)

> Breakfast here is… *salty* tea. I'm Seetu, a pressure cooker, and I'm eating with a family in every district of India. Stop one: Leh, Ladakh! Many homes start with gur-gur cha: tea churned with butter and salt… with khambir bread, or kholak: roasted barley mixed right into the tea. No cooking. Fuel is precious up here. Spice? Zero puffs. Lunch in many homes? Rice, dal and vegetables. Every day… or just for guests? *(Host: Every day!)* No no no… okay. Just one more. Dinner: a steaming bowl of thukpa, noodle soup with vegetables, sometimes meat. Up here, hot soup isn't a treat. It's survival. Mind blown: researchers found rice used to be a luxury in Ladakh. Today, cheap subsidised rice from the plains is everyday food, and local barley is fading. Three whistles — done! Just one more… Changthang!

**Native-script dish names:** left as placeholders on purpose. I won't guess Bhoti/Tibetan spellings; the Ladakhi reviewer supplies them.

**Pronunciation:**
- gur-gur cha → "goor-goor chah"
- khambir → "kham-BEER"
- kholak → "KHO-lak"
- thukpa → "THOOK-pah"
- Changthang → "CHANG-thang"
- Have the Ladakhi reviewer confirm all of these.

**The host voice "Every day!"** is a 1-word line. It can be the VO actor in a different register, or skipped and shown as text instead. **Never** imitate a specific community's accent.
