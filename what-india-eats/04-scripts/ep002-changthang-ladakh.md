## Clean VO

> A whole yak… in one winter?! I'm Seetu, eating through every district of India. Stop two: Changthang, land of nomad herders. Breakfast: tsampa, roasted barley flour, stirred into salty butter tea. Spice? Still zero puffs. Lunch, in many nomad homes, is meat and dairy: yak or mutton stew, and churpe, dried cheese. Ow! Okay… you soften it in tea. Dinner? Like much of Ladakh: thukpa, warm noodle soup. Again? Yes, please! Mind blown: researchers found a nomad family may eat one yak, or three to four sheep or goats, each winter. And these herders are among India's top pashmina producers: cashmere wool! Three whistles — done! Next stop… Nubra!
